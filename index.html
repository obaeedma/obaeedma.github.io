Profile

Analytical by training, curious by nature.

I'm M A Obaeed — a data analyst who believes every dataset carries a story worth telling honestly.

I grew up in Narayanganj, where a love of books, debate and science fairs shaped how I think: ask good questions, check the evidence, then explain it so anyone can follow. That instinct led me to Computer Science & Engineering at BRAC University.

Today I work with Python, SQL, Excel and Power BI to clean messy data, find the patterns that matter and present them clearly. Across 25+ academic and independent projects — from air-quality analysis to data-modelled web platforms and dialectal Bangla NLP research — I've learned that good analysis is as much about care and clarity as it is about code.

Beyond the screen, I've founded and led clubs, run events, coordinated sponsors and spoken on stage. And whenever I can, I'm out with a camera — I've explored all 64 districts of Bangladesh. Curiosity is the thread that ties it all together.

Undergraduate Thesis · NLP & Machine Learning

Detecting hate speech in dialectal Bangla.

Dialectal Bangla Hate Speech Detection using Natural Language Processing · BIDWESH dataset

Most Bangla NLP work assumes the standard written form. Online comments don't. This research builds and evaluates models on regional dialects, where vocabulary, spelling and class balance all work against you.

Noakhalidialect variety
Barishaldialect variety

Collect

~9,183 dialectal comments across 14 classes (BIDWESH).

Preprocess

Cleaning and normalising noisy dialectal text.

Balance

Class weighting to counter label imbalance.

Model

Deep and classical models trained side by side.

Evaluate

Macro-F1, precision, recall and per-class analysis.
